> During my participation in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, I had the opportunity to explore cloud computing technologies, improve my technical skills, and apply my academic knowledge to a practical project. The following are my reflections, experiences, and suggestions regarding the internship program.

### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The FCAJ program provided a learning environment that encouraged independent research, practical exercises, and continuous improvement. During the project, I had opportunities to explore AWS services, configure cloud infrastructure, and resolve technical issues. These activities helped me become more familiar with practical working methods and develop a more proactive learning attitude.

**2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team**

The guidance and support from mentors and the admin team helped me understand the project requirements and determine appropriate directions for technical research. The opportunity to study independently, practice configurations, and troubleshoot issues also helped me improve my self-learning and problem-solving abilities.

**3. Relevance of the Work to My Field of Study**

The internship activities were closely related to my major in Computer Networks and Data Communications. Through the project **Research and Implementation of Infrastructure Security on AWS**, I applied my knowledge of network architecture, IP addressing, routing, and access control. I also gained practical experience with AWS services such as VPC, EC2, RDS, Application Load Balancer, and AWS WAF.

**4. Learning Opportunities and Skills Development**

The internship provided opportunities to study and practice various AWS services, including VPC, EC2, RDS, Security Groups, Application Load Balancer, AWS WAF, CloudWatch, CloudTrail, and AWS Config. In addition to technical knowledge, I improved my ability to read technical documentation, follow implementation guides, troubleshoot configuration issues, and document technical procedures.

**5. Company Culture and Teamwork**

Participating in FCAJ activities helped me understand the importance of communication, knowledge sharing, and teamwork. Through meetings and community activities, I had opportunities to exchange ideas, learn from other participants, and improve my ability to collaborate in a technical learning environment.

**6. Policies and Benefits for Interns**

I appreciate the learning opportunities, technical guidance, and practical experience provided by the FCAJ program. The program allowed me to explore cloud technologies and apply them to a project related to my major. These experiences helped me gain a clearer understanding of my professional interests and future career direction.

### Additional Questions

**1. What did I appreciate most during the internship?**

What I appreciated most was the opportunity to research and implement a practical AWS infrastructure security project. Through configuring VPC networking, deploying an EC2 instance, establishing connectivity with Amazon RDS, configuring Security Groups, and integrating AWS WAF with an Application Load Balancer, I gained a better understanding of how cloud infrastructure components work together.

**2. What do I think the program should improve for future interns?**

I hope the program can continue providing detailed documentation and step-by-step technical guidance for each project stage. More practical examples, technical discussions, and periodic progress reviews would also help interns understand requirements, address difficulties, and receive feedback during implementation.

**3. Would I recommend this program to my friends? Why?**

Yes. I would recommend the FCAJ program to students who want to explore information technology, especially cloud computing and computer networking. The program provides opportunities to learn new technologies, participate in practical activities, exchange knowledge, and develop independent research skills.

### Suggestions and Expectations

**1. Suggestions for Improving the Internship Experience**

I hope the program will continue providing practical projects with learning paths from basic to advanced levels. Additional technical sessions on AWS architecture, cloud security, network configuration, and infrastructure monitoring would help interns strengthen their knowledge and gain more practical experience.

**2. Interest in Continuing the Program in the Future**

I would welcome further opportunities to participate in FCAJ learning activities, technical workshops, and community events. These activities would allow me to continue improving my knowledge of AWS cloud computing, computer networking, and infrastructure security.

**3. Other Comments**

I would like to sincerely thank FCAJ, the mentors, and the organizing team for providing me with opportunities to learn, practice, and gain valuable experience throughout the program. The knowledge and skills I have gained will serve as an important foundation for my personal development, career orientation, and future professional work.
